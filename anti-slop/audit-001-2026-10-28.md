## Findings

| # | Prio | Rule | Where | Finding |
|---|------|------|-------|---------|
| 1 | HIGH | C-4 / data loss | `Services/ConfigSession.cs:31`, `Core/Configuration/ConfigService.cs:64` | If `config.json` has a JSON error, `Config` holds defaults and `LoadError` is set. Any edit in the UI then runs `Update` → `Save(defaults + edit)` and overwrites the user's file. `JsonFileStore` promises "a typo never wipes the user's work"; the app path breaks that. Fix: reject `Update` while `LoadError` is set (one guard, plus a test). |
| 2 | HIGH | R-25 (non-text 3:1) | `Themes/Styles.axaml:125`, `Tokens.axaml` `SelectionBrush` | Orange selection ring around a key sits on `LidWellBrush`: 1.16:1 light, 1.37:1 dark. Around OLED/fader on `LidBrush`: 1.51:1 light, 1.05:1 dark. Visible by hue only; fails for colour-blind users. |
| 3 | HIGH | R-32 (focus visible) | `Styles.axaml:180`, `:166` | Focus ring uses `InkBrush`. Dark theme on lid (OLED, faders): 2.40:1, below 3:1. In the key well: 3.12:1 dark (pass, tight), 5.05:1 light. |
| 4 | HIGH | R-25 (text 4.5:1) | `Views/PadView.axaml:88-89` | Key number and gesture hint use `KeycapNumberBrush` on the keycap: 2.46:1 light, 2.39:1 dark, at 11–12.5px. |
| 5 | MEDIUM | R-32 (unverified) | `Styles.axaml:37-58` | `Button.tab` replaces the whole template and has no `:focus-visible` style. Whether Fluent's focus adorner still shows was not checked in the running app. |
| 6 | LOW | R-25 (non-text) | `MainWindow.axaml:15`, `OfflineBrush` | Offline dot 2.42–2.73:1 light. Text next to it carries the state, so impact is small. |
| 7 | LOW | R-39 (state not colour-only) | `Views/KeyInspectorView.axaml:28` | "Gesture is bound" is a 6px orange dot only (3.17:1), no text or accessible name. |
